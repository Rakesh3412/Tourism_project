Rajasthan

THE LAND OF KINGS ♔ 🐪

Rajasthan is a land of rich culture, heritage and royal history. Known for its magnificent palaces, majestic forts, golden deserts, vibrant festivals and warm hospitality, Rajasthan is one of India's most popular tourist destinations.

TOP TOURIST PLACES

Jaipur

The Pink City famous for its palaces, forts & vibrant bazaars.

Jodhpur

The Blue City known for the mighty Mehrangarh Fort.

Udaipur

The City of Lakes and beautiful royal palaces.

Jaisalmer

The Golden City and stunning desert landscapes.

Pushkar

Famous for Pushkar Lake and Brahma Temple.

EXPLORE RAJASTHAN

Culture & Heritage

Experience the royal heritage, colorful festivals, folk music, dance and traditions.

Adventure & Desert Safari

Enjoy thrilling desert safaris, camel rides, paramotoring and camping.

Food & Shopping

Taste the royal Rajasthani cuisine and shop handicrafts, textiles and jewelry.

Fairs & Festivals

Pushkar Fair, Desert Festival, Teej, Gangaur and many more.
